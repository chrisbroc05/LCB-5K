Support the LCB 5K

Two ways to help families facing cancer — support the event or donate directly to Family Reach.

Support the LCB 5K

Unable to attend? You can still make a contribution to support the LCB 5K and help us cover event costs while maximizing our impact for families facing cancer.

Contributions made through the LCB 5K website support the LCB 5K and are not tax-deductible charitable donations. Following the event, LCB will make a lump-sum donation to Family Reach.

Donate Directly to Family Reach

Want to make a tax-deductible charitable donation? Donate directly to Family Reach through their website.

When donating, select "In Honor Of" and enter "LCB 5K" so your gift is credited to our event. Family Reach handles the charitable donation and tax receipt.
