Our Mission

Every Step Makes a Difference

The LCB 5K exists to help families facing the financial and emotional challenges that come with cancer. When a loved one is diagnosed, the costs go far beyond treatment — housing, food, utilities, and transportation all become harder to manage.

Every registration and donation helps families focus on healing instead of bills.

All proceeds benefit Family Reach, a nonprofit dedicated to removing the financial barriers between cancer patients and their treatment — helping with essentials like housing, food, utilities, and transportation.

Love. Care. Believe.

Together, we turn miles into meaningful support for families in need.

Our Story

Running in Their Memory

This event was created in memory of two beloved uncles whose strength, love, and legacy continue to inspire the LCB 5K every year.

Kenneth Angelino

Father, husband, son, grandfather, brother, and friend. A master barber who was dedicated, loyal, humble, and courageous. Ken's light still shines in everyone who knew and loved him.

Vincent Evola

Known as Enzo — an amazing son, father, friend, and the best brother a sister could hope for. His kindness, strength, and unwavering love made a profound impact on all of us.
