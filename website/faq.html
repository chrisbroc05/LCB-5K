Everything you need to know about the LCB 5K 2026.

The LCB 5K will take place at Mallard Lake Forest Preserve, 26W580 Schick Rd, Hanover Park, IL 60133. Our path takes us along the water and through scenic trails.

Check-in begins at 9:30 AM. The race starts at 10:30 AM on Saturday, October 3rd, 2026. We recommend arriving early to allow time for parking and check-in.

We strongly encourage registering in advance to ensure we have your t-shirt size and enough supplies. Day-of registration may be available if space allows — contact us for details.

Free parking is available at Mallard Lake Forest Preserve. Follow event signage on race day for the designated parking areas closest to check-in.

The LCB 5K is held to raise funds to support families facing the financial and emotional burdens of cancer through Family Reach. The LCB 5K collects registration fees and event contributions, uses funds to cover the costs associated with putting on the event, and makes a lump-sum donation to Family Reach following the event.

You can donate directly through Family Reach's website. When making your donation, select "In Honor Of" and enter "LCB 5K" so Family Reach can properly allocate the contribution to the LCB 5K. Family Reach handles the charitable donation and tax receipt.

Checks can be made payable to Family Reach. Please note "LCB 5K" on the check so Family Reach can properly allocate the funds to the event. Mail to:

Family Reach
142 Berkeley St, Suite 310
Boston, MA 02116

Family Reach is the nonprofit beneficiary of the LCB 5K and a 501(c)(3) organization that provides financial assistance to families facing cancer — helping with housing, food, utilities, and transportation so families can focus on healing instead of bills.
