Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the LCB 5K 2026.

The LCB 5K will take place at Mallard Lake Forest Preserve, 26W580 Schick Rd, Hanover Park, IL 60133. Our path takes us along the water and through scenic trails.

Check-in begins at 9:30 AM. The race starts at 10:30 AM on Saturday, October 3rd, 2026. We recommend arriving early to allow time for parking and check-in.

We strongly encourage registering in advance to ensure we have your t-shirt size and enough supplies. Day-of registration may be available if space allows — contact us for details.

Free parking is available at Mallard Lake Forest Preserve. Follow event signage on race day for the designated parking areas closest to check-in.

Yes! This is a family-friendly event. Strollers are welcome on the course. Walkers, runners, and families are all encouraged to participate.

Dogs are allowed at the forest preserve but must be on leashes under 10 feet long. Please clean up after your pet and bring water for them too.

The event will take place rain or shine, unless severe weather makes it unsafe. Any changes will be posted on our website and social media channels.

Donations support the Family Reach Foundation, which provides financial assistance to families battling cancer — helping with housing, food, utilities, and transportation. All donations are pooled and sent as a single contribution to Family Reach following the event.
